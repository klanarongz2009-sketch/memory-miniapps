# Project notice

Memory MiniApp catalog is a personal indie project by [klanarongz2009-sketch](https://github.com/klanarongz2009-sketch). Please read this before you rely on it, open a pull request, or post in Discussions.

## In short

- **One maintainer, limited access:** the project is run by one maintainer who is a boarding-school student. Access to devices and to this repository is limited and irregular.
- **Slow or no replies:** issues, pull requests, Discussions, complaints, security reports, takedown requests and other urgent contacts may take days or longer to be read, and some may not be answered.
- **May pause or disappear:** the repository and the catalog site may be paused, suspended or removed, including when the maintainer regains access and reviews it.
- **Bots are not the maintainer:** the Discussion bots may answer first, but they cannot merge pull requests or make decisions.
- **Free, as is:** the catalog and the apps are free and provided as is, without warranty of any kind.

## Maintainer availability and urgent reports

- **Limited access:** while at school the maintainer often has no access to devices, the project's accounts or its contact channels. Access returns only when he is back.
- **Urgent reports wait:** a complaint, security report, takedown request, platform report or any other urgent contact may wait until he is back. If something here affects you, open an issue anyway; it will be seen when he returns.
- **No guarantees:** there is no guaranteed response time, review time or uptime.

## Discussion bots

Three AI bots answer in [Discussions](https://github.com/klanarongz2009-sketch/memory-miniapps/discussions). Every bot post starts with its name.

- **Zap-Mem** (runs on Zapia): checks text and messages.
- **Code** (runs on Sesame): reviews the code of the app files.
- **IdeasBox** (runs on Wabi): posts ideas for AIs.

The bots may reply before the maintainer does. They cannot merge pull requests, approve changes, accept legal notices, security reports or takedown requests, or make decisions for the maintainer. A bot reply is not a promise that anything will be merged or changed. See the pinned [Bots Discussion](https://github.com/klanarongz2009-sketch/memory-miniapps/discussions/1).

## Why the project may pause or close

- **Maintainer away:** updates, reviews and replies stop while the maintainer has no access.
- **Closed without a stated reason:** the project may close without an explanation.
- **A claim about content:** if there is a claim about any app or text here, the affected part, or the whole repository, may be taken down.
- **A platform change:** a free service the project or its bots depend on (GitHub Pages, Zapia, Sesame, Wabi) may change, limit or stop.

## Content and privacy

- The apps are built by AI assistants from the public, privacy-filtered English text of an AI assistant's memory. Entries that were mostly personal are shown in a general form and marked "generalized".
- No personal information about the maintainer is published here, and none should be added: no real names, contact details, location, school, dates of birth, accounts, passwords or tokens. Pull requests or posts that add personal information will not be accepted.
- Discussions and pull requests are public. Do not post personal information there.
